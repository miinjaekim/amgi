# TOPIK Advanced Vocabulary Pack — Draft for Review

**Concept:** the Korean counterpart to the TOEIC pack — one curated pool (160 words) aimed squarely at **TOPIK II 고급, 5–6급**. Browsed and drawn from over time, not imported in one shot. A `lookup` pack, so tapping a word runs the normal Learn flow: most entries are Sino-Korean, and the hanja breakdown the Korean explanation already writes is doing real work on words like 여건 or 취지.

**Selection filter:** the register TOPIK II actually tests, not more everyday nouns. A learner who can hold a conversation still meets these cold in a 신문 기사, a 강연, or their own 쓰기 54번 answer. Words a 3급 learner already has (회사, 준비하다, 기분) are deliberately out — the filter is the TOEIC one pointed the other way.

**Context hints (48 of 160):** three jobs. Homographs where the everyday sense would win (경기, 미치다, 지나치다, 자원); everyday verbs whose *written* sense is the tested one (밝히다, 꼽히다, 치르다); and every idiom, because 발이 넓다 explained literally is a sentence about feet and 어부지리 glossed character by character is four unrelated hanja.

**Sources:** the 고급 tier of 국립국어원's published learner word lists ([「한국어 학습용 어휘 목록」](https://www.korean.go.kr), and the later 「한국어 교육 어휘 내용 개발」 4단계 고급 list), cross-read against released TOPIK II 읽기·듣기 papers from [국립국제교육원](https://www.topik.go.kr) and the 고급 vocabulary of the standard university series (연세·서강·서울대 5–6급). **Assembled by hand from those, not scraped** — which is exactly why this file exists: the list is a draft until you've read it.

## 1. 시사·사회 — current affairs & society (30)

격차 · 양극화 · 고령화 · 저출산 · 복지 · 정책 · 규제 · 여론 · 물가 *(hint: the general level of prices)* · 경기 *(hint: the state of the economy, not a sports match)* · 침체 · 실업률 · 고용 · 소비 · 유통 · 수요 · 공급 · 부작용 · 실태 · 추세 · 현황 · 대책 · 방안 · 논란 · 우려 · 갈등 · 훼손 *(hint: damage to what should have been kept intact)* · 자원 *(hint: natural or human resources, not volunteering)* · 차별 · 소외

The standing subject matter of 읽기 and 듣기 above 4급, and the vocabulary a 쓰기 53번 chart description is written in.

## 2. 추상 개념 — the nouns an argument is built from (30)

인식 · 관점 · 편견 · 근거 · 요인 · 여건 · 취지 · 성향 · 경향 · 역량 · 자질 · 잠재력 · 효율 · 배려 · 성찰 · 의의 *(hint: significance, as in 역사적 의의)* · 전제 *(hint: a premise or precondition)* · 맥락 · 측면 · 속성 *(hint: an inherent property)* · 비중 *(hint: the share something accounts for)* · 수단 · 기준 · 지표 *(hint: an indicator or index)* · 대안 · 한계 · 모순 · 타당성 · 균형 · 정서 *(hint: shared sentiment, as in 국민 정서)*

What makes a 고급 읽기 passage hard: the grammar is plain and every load-bearing word is one of these.

## 3. 고급 동사 (40)

초래하다 · 야기하다 · 유발하다 · 좌우하다 · 도모하다 · 모색하다 · 시사하다 · 감안하다 · 부각되다 · 대두되다 · 급증하다 · 완화하다 · 촉진하다 · 억제하다 · 반영하다 · 지속되다 · 위축되다 · 정착하다 · 도입하다 · 확산되다 · 추진하다 · 보완하다 · 축소하다 · 겪다 · 이루어지다 · 기여하다 · 극복하다 · 지적하다 · 촉구하다 · 뒷받침하다 · 헤아리다 · 아우르다

…plus eight everyday verbs carried by their written sense, all hinted:

| word | context hint |
|---|---|
| 미치다 | to reach or have an effect on, as in 영향을 미치다 — not "to go crazy" |
| 차지하다 | to occupy or account for a share, as in 절반을 차지하다 |
| 밝히다 | to state or reveal something officially |
| 살피다 | to examine something carefully |
| 다루다 | to handle or deal with a topic or an issue |
| 꼽히다 | to be named or counted as, as in 대표적인 사례로 꼽히다 |
| 치르다 | to hold or go through an event, or to pay a price |
| 마련하다 | to arrange or put in place, as in 대책을 마련하다 |

## 4. 형용사·묘사 (20)

뚜렷하다 · 미미하다 · 막대하다 · 무분별하다 · 사소하다 · 원활하다 · 저조하다 · 활발하다 · 두드러지다 · 불가피하다 · 바람직하다 · 부당하다 · 타당하다 · 모호하다 · 절실하다 · 냉정하다 · 까다롭다 · 번거롭다 · 무모하다 · 지나치다 *(hint: to be excessive — the same form also means "to pass by")*

How a 고급 passage grades something. 미미하다 vs 막대하다 is often the whole answer to a 읽기 문제.

## 5. 부사·연결 표현 (20)

오히려 · 비로소 *(hint: only then, after something finally made it possible)* · 굳이 · 어차피 · 좀처럼 *(hint: hardly ever — with a negative ending)* · 자칫 *(hint: at the slightest slip, usually with -(으)면)* · 하물며 · 도리어 · 이왕이면 · 마침내 · 새삼 · 무려 *(hint: as many as)* · 다만 *(hint: however, adding one qualification)* · 아울러 · 나아가 *(hint: furthermore, extending to something broader)* · 반면 *(hint: on the other hand — 반면(에))* · 이에 따라 *(hint: accordingly)* · 이른바 · 이를테면 · 심지어

The half of 쓰기 54번 that isn't vocabulary: what makes an answer read as 고급 is how its sentences are joined. Also the standard 빈칸 answers in 읽기.

## Open questions

- **Is 고급-only the right cut?** The pack assumes the 3–4급 layer is already there. A 중급 companion (`topik-intermediate`) is a second registry entry and nothing else, if the jump is too steep in practice.
- **Any words to cut/add?** Sections 5 and 6 are the ones to argue with — 부사 and 관용구 are the least list-like and the most editorial.
- **Phrase entries.** 이에 따라, 반면 and the 관용구 are multi-word, which the pack format allows but the TOEIC pack never exercised. Confirm they read well as Learn results before this section grows.
- **Drill/review.** A `lookup` pack still has no deck review — words saved from here land in My Cards, not a TOPIK collection, until `packId` is stamped through the Learn flow (see backlog). That is felt more here than on TOEIC, because a 6급 target is a long study loop.
