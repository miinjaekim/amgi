## 1. Beta App Description (베타 앱 설명)

### 한국어

```
Amgi는 언어 학습용 플래시카드 앱입니다.

단어를 찾아보면 뜻과 함께 뉘앙스, 격식, 실제 쓰임, 예문이 나옵니다. 한자가
있는 단어는 한자 풀이도 함께 나옵니다. 그 설명을 그대로 카드로 저장하면,
간격 반복(spaced repetition) 알고리즘이 정한 시점에 다시 나타납니다.

글 모드도 있습니다. 몇 문장 쓰면 원어민이라면 어떻게 썼을지 보여주고,
무엇이 달라졌는지 짚어 줍니다. 거기서 걸린 문법은 문법 패턴으로 따로
연습할 수 있습니다. 처음에는 빈칸을 채우다가, 익숙해지면 직접 문장을
씁니다.

설명과 첨삭은 AI가 만들기 때문에 틀릴 수 있습니다. 어색하거나 잘못된 부분을
알려 주시는 것이 이번 베타에서 가장 도움이 됩니다.

지원 학습 언어: 영어, 일본어, 중국어(번체), 스웨덴어, 프랑스어, 한국어
앱 화면 언어: 한국어 / 영어 (설정에서 변경)

베타 기간 동안 무료이며, 광고나 트래킹은 없습니다.
```

### English

```
Amgi is a flashcard app for language learners.

Look up a word and you get an explanation of it: the meaning, plus nuance,
register, how it is actually used, and example sentences. Words written in
Chinese characters also get a character breakdown. Save that explanation as a
card and it comes back on a spaced-repetition schedule.

There is also a passage mode. Write a few sentences and you get them back as a
native speaker would have written them, with notes on what changed. Grammar
that trips you up there becomes a pattern you practise separately — filling in
a gap at first, writing your own sentence once it sticks.

The explanations and the writing feedback are generated by an AI, so they can
be wrong. Telling me when they are is the most useful thing you can do in this
beta.

Study languages: English, Japanese, Traditional Chinese, Swedish, French, Korean
App display language: Korean or English (change in Settings)

Free during the beta. No ads, no tracking.
```

## 4. What to Test (테스트할 내용)

### 한국어

```
며칠 편하게 써 보시면서 단어를 배우고, 복습하고, 카드를 관리하는 흐름을
한 번씩 사용해 봐 주세요. AI 설명이 어색하거나 틀린 경우, 앱이 멈추거나
튕기는 경우, 언어 설정과 다르게 화면이 표시되는 경우를 알려 주시면 큰
도움이 됩니다.

이번 빌드에서 새로 확인해 보시면 좋은 것들:
· 문법 패턴 — 이번 빌드에서 가장 많이 바뀐 부분입니다. 글을 첨삭받으면
  걸린 문법을 패턴으로 저장할 수 있고, 카드 화면 위쪽 '문법'에서 직접
  추가할 수도 있습니다. 복습에 들어가면 처음에는 빈칸 채우기로 나오고,
  두 번 맞히면 상황을 주고 직접 문장을 쓰게 합니다. 빈칸 문제가 말이
  되는지, 채점이 맞는 답을 틀렸다고 하지는 않는지 봐 주세요. 틀렸다고
  나왔는데 본인 답도 맞다고 생각되면 '내 답도 맞아요'를 눌러 주세요.
· 내 카드 화면 — 단어팩에서 저장한 카드가 이제 '내 카드'에도 보입니다.
  예전에는 팩 카드라는 이유로 목록에서 빠져 있었어요. 필터 버튼 하나에
  덱·보관 상태·정렬이 다 들어갔으니, 지금 뭐가 켜져 있는지 버튼만 보고
  알 수 있는지 봐 주세요. 내보내기는 화면에 보이는 그대로 나갑니다.
· 군사용어 팩 두 개 — 부대·참모, 안보·정세. 한국어↔영어 양방향이라
  학습 언어가 영어여도 한국어여도 보입니다. 번역이 틀렸거나 실제로 쓰는
  말과 다르면 꼭 알려 주세요. 이 팩은 특히 그런 지적이 필요합니다.
· 맞춤법 — 오타를 내고 찾아보면 '이 단어로 찾았어요'가 뜨고, 원래 친
  대로 찾을 수도 있습니다. 멀쩡한 단어를 틀렸다고 고치지는 않는지
  (사투리, 옛말, 고유명사, 활용형) 봐 주시면 좋습니다.
· 품사 — 새로 찾아본 단어에는 명사·동사 같은 품사가 붙습니다. 모국어
  설정을 따라가니 한국어면 '명사', 영어면 'Noun'으로 보여야 합니다.
  예전에 저장한 카드와 팩 카드에는 안 붙는 게 정상입니다.
· 복습 중 보관·삭제 — 복습하다가 카드를 보관하거나 지우면 그 카드는
  같은 세션에서 다시 나오지 않아야 합니다. 다시 나오면 알려 주세요.
· 글 첨삭 복사 — 고쳐 준 문장 옆 복사 버튼을 눌러 다른 앱에 붙여넣어
  보세요. 이번 빌드에 새로 들어간 기능입니다.

그밖에, 이번이 오랜만의 새 빌드라 아직 실제 앱에서 확인 못 한 것들이
있습니다. 발음 듣기, 카드 내보내기(CSV·Anki), 공유, 인터넷 없이 복습하기,
복습 알림이 실제로 오는지 — 이 중 안 되는 게 있으면 알려 주세요.

피드백은 TestFlight의 '피드백 보내기' 또는 kenyamjkim@gmail.com으로 보내주세요.
```

### English

```
Please use the app for a few days and try the flow of learning a word,
reviewing it, and managing your cards. It would help a lot if you flag any
AI explanations that seem wrong or awkward, any freezes or crashes, or
screens showing the wrong language for your settings.

New in this build, worth a look:
· Grammar patterns — the largest change in this build. Get a passage
  reviewed and you can keep the grammar it caught as a pattern; you can
  also add one by hand under "Grammar" at the top of the Cards screen.
  In Review it starts as a fill-in-the-gap, and after two clean passes it
  gives you a situation and asks you to write the sentence yourself. Please
  check that the gap exercises make sense and that the grading doesn't mark
  a correct answer wrong. If it does, tap "My answer was right too".
· My Cards — words you saved from a pack now show up here. They used to be
  dropped from the list just for belonging to a pack. Deck, archived status
  and sort all moved behind one filter button: can you tell what's currently
  on just by looking at it? Export gives you exactly what's on screen.
· Two military terminology packs — Unit & Staff, and Security Affairs.
  Korean↔English in both directions, so they appear whether you study
  English or Korean. Please flag any translation that's wrong or isn't what
  people actually say — this pack needs that more than the others.
· Spellcheck — misspell something and look it up. You should get "showing
  results for…" with a way back to what you typed. Watch for it correcting
  words that were fine: dialect, archaic words, proper nouns, inflections.
· Part of speech — newly looked-up words get a noun/verb badge. It follows
  your native language, so "Noun" in English and "명사" in Korean. Older
  cards and pack cards having none is expected.
· Archive and delete during review — archive or delete a card mid-session
  and it shouldn't come back later in that same session. Tell us if it does.
· Copying the rewrite — there's a copy button next to your corrected
  passage. New in this build; try pasting it somewhere.

One more thing: it's been a while since the last build, so a few things have
never been checked on a real install. Pronunciation audio, exporting cards
(CSV/Anki), sharing, reviewing with no connection, and whether the review
reminder actually arrives. Let us know if any of them fall over.

Send feedback via TestFlight's "Send Beta Feedback" or to
kenyamjkim@gmail.com.
```

**Review Notes:**

```
Amgi is a language-learning app that pairs AI-generated word explanations with
spaced-repetition flashcards.

Sign-in: The app uses Google Sign-In (Firebase Authentication) only. Demo
credentials are provided in the demo account fields above. Any Google account can
sign in and immediately access all functionality.

How to test: On first launch the app asks for a native language and a study
language before anything else, and cannot be used until both are answered. This is
required because explanations are written in the native language, and a learner
cannot study the language they already speak. Both can be changed later in Settings.

On the Learn tab, type any word or phrase and tap Learn to get an explanation, then
save it as a flashcard. The Word/Passage toggle at the top of the same tab switches
to writing review: enter a few sentences and the app returns a native-sounding
rewrite plus specific notes on grammar and phrasing. The Review tab runs the
spaced-repetition session over saved cards. The Cards tab lists, edits, and exports
saved cards; its Cards/Grammar toggle switches to grammar patterns, which are
practised in Review as short written exercises rather than flipped as cards. The
Packs tab holds pre-made decks that can be saved as cards or drilled directly.

Account deletion: Settings → Delete account, at the bottom of the screen. This
permanently deletes the account and all associated data from within the app, as
required by guideline 5.1.1(v). It asks for confirmation and may re-prompt for
Google sign-in, because deleting an account requires a recent authentication.

Notifications: The app can schedule local reminders for the word of the day and
for due reviews. Both are off by default and are turned on individually in
Settings; the permission prompt appears only when one is enabled. These are local
notifications scheduled on the device — the app sends no remote push and stores no
push tokens.

Third-party processing: Word explanations and writing review are generated with
Google's Gemini API, and pronunciation audio with Google Cloud Text-to-Speech. Only
the submitted text, optional context, and language settings are sent — never account
identifiers. This is described in the privacy policy.

The app contains no ads, no analytics, and no tracking. It does not access location,
contacts, camera, or photos.

Privacy policy: https://amgi-iota.vercel.app/privacy
```
